Business question & goals

This project analyzes full-year 2015 retail transaction records from a public Kaggle pizza sales dataset covering 21,350 orders, 49,574 pizzas sold, $817.86K in total revenue, and a $38.31 average order value.

The operational goal is to support ingredient inventory planning, peak-period staff scheduling, and targeted promotional bundles by identifying peak demand periods, top and bottom menu performers, and customer preferences across categories and sizes.

Analysis pipeline

Workflow from raw CSV preparation in Power Query through SQL querying to Excel and Power BI dashboards.

Step 01

Raw CSV

Inspected 2015 order lines including order timestamps, pizza names, categories (Classic, Supreme, Veggie, Chicken), sizes, quantities, and prices.

Step 02

Excel & Power Query

Cleaned and shaped transaction tables using Power Query and summarized sales breakdowns with Excel Pivot Tables.

Step 03

SQL queries

Wrote SQL queries (PostgreSQL / SQL Server) to compute core KPIs, daily/monthly/hourly trends, category and size shares, and top/bottom items.

Step 04

Power BI dashboard

Built interactive Excel and Power BI dashboards with DAX measures structured around a 60-30-10 visual color hierarchy.

Key findings

Full-year 2015 performance across 21,350 orders and $817.86K total revenue.

The operation sold 49,574 pizzas across 21,350 orders in 2015, generating $817.86K in revenue at a $38.31 average order value.

49,574

Total pizzas sold across 21,350 customer orders during 2015, establishing baseline ingredient demand and an average basket size of $38.31.

Order demand peaks on Fridays followed by Thursdays, with the Classic category and Large size leading customer orders.

Across the four menu categories (Classic, Supreme, Veggie, Chicken), Classic generates the largest share, while Large is the preferred pizza size—directly informing dough prep and end-of-week staffing schedules.

The Thai Chicken Pizza generates the highest total revenue, while The Brie Carre Pizza records the lowest revenue on the menu.

Separating top revenue contributors from the lowest-performing item helps prioritize ingredient availability and evaluate promotional bundling opportunities.

Monthly and hourly order distributions reveal clear seasonal and intraday demand windows for shift scheduling.
